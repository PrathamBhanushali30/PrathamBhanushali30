## 🧭 About Me

> *"I build security solutions at the intersection of offensive security, artificial intelligence, and industrial cybersecurity — not as separate disciplines, but as one connected practice."*

Hello! I am a multifaceted cybersecurity professional dedicated to securing complex environments across the digital and physical spectrum. While I have recently transitioned into a specialized OT Security role within the professional workforce, my technical foundation is built upon a comprehensive understanding of the entire cybersecurity landscape. 

My expertise spans **Offensive Security (VAPT, Mobile/Web AppSec)**, **Defensive Operations (SOC, Network Monitoring)**, and **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)** frameworks. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as purely an academic concept, I leverage it to build robust, automated security workflows, AI-assisted vulnerability scanners, and real-time defensive mechanisms for both enterprise IT and critical OT/ICS infrastructures. My approach ensures that security is holistic, compliant, and actively resilient against modern threats.
